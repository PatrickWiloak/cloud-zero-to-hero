## Which HashiCorp exam should I take first?

Start with Terraform. Take the exams in this order:

1. **[Terraform Associate (004)](terraform-associate/)** - the foundation exam. Study plan: [strategy.md](terraform-associate/strategy.md).
2. **[Terraform Authoring and Operations Advanced](terraform-authoring-operations-pro/)** - the hands-on, lab-based exam. HashiCorp strongly recommends the Terraform Associate (or equivalent experience) first. Study plan: [strategy.md](terraform-authoring-operations-pro/strategy.md).

HashiCorp also lists a Terraform Infrastructure Engineer Professional exam and two Vault exams (Vault Associate and Vault Operations Advanced). This repo has no guide for those yet.

HashiCorp's certifications page no longer lists a Consul, Packer or Nomad exam; those guides are kept as product study notes: [Consul](consul-associate/), [Packer](packer-associate/) and [Nomad](nomad-associate/).

| Guide | Format | Duration | Cost | Validity |
|-------|--------|----------|------|----------|
| [Terraform Associate (004)](terraform-associate/) | Multiple choice, online proctored | 1 hour | $70.50 USD, plus locally applicable taxes and fees; free retake not included | 2 years |
| [Terraform Authoring and Operations Advanced](terraform-authoring-operations-pro/) | Lab-based scenarios plus multiple choice, online proctored | 4 hours, with one 15-minute break | $295 USD plus taxes, with a free retake included | 2 years |
